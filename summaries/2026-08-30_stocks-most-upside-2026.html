Negative Beta Stocks as Hedge

  • ~25% of the S&P 500 has negative beta (surprising fact)
  • These stocks correlate inversely to the S&P day-to-day — a natural hedge without buying puts
  • Examples: McDonald's (MCD), PepsiCo (PEP), utilities, insurance
  • Analyst argument: Index put hedges have repeatedly burned people — negative beta stocks give index-like diversification without the cost of options

Jeremy's Hedging Strategy

  • Would love the market to rally hard through the rest of 2026 → then buy put options on the QQQ at year-end or early 2027 to protect against 2027 risks
  • Simplest hedge for non-advanced investors: Hold a bit more cash
  • Very bullish on under-$20B market cap stocks — "real economy" type names — through 2027–2029 even if large caps face headwinds
