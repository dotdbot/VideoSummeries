🟠 What Cloudflare Actually Built

AI Crawl Control

See which AI crawlers are hitting your site. Allow specific crawlers, block others, understand who's using your content. Full visibility and control.

Pay Per Crawl (X42 / HTTP 402)

Site owners charge AI crawlers per access. Crawler sends payment intent → if refused, gets a 402 "payment required" response with price → pays and retries. Cloudflare verifies at the edge.

Monetization Gateway

Applies beyond crawlers to any resource behind Cloudflare: web pages, datasets, APIs, MCP tool calls, premium endpoints, files, search indexes. Payment becomes part of the request itself.

AI Index / Agent-Readable Infrastructure

Tools like LLM.txt, MCP servers, search APIs, bulk data APIs — making websites structured enough for agents to use. Agents need clean doors; humans can tolerate messy websites.

The request becomes the transaction. No checkout flow. No account creation. No sales call. No enterprise procurement. A tiny resource on the internet becomes a business. A data set charges per lookup. An API charges per successful call. A research archive charges per answer.

2
Agent Readiness for Businesses
"SEO" for the agent internet — make companies easy for agents to understand, trust, compare & recommend
📖 The Problem
When someone asks their AI assistant "find me the best payroll provider for a 15-person company in CA," the agent has to understand: Who is this for? What does it cost? What does it replace? What integrations? What are the risks?

Most websites make this impossible: hidden pricing, buried docs, stale pages, important info in PDFs from 2002, marketing speak like "unlocking operational excellence."
💰 The Wedge — Paid Audit
Run 20–50 buyer-intent prompts across major AI tools: "What is the best software for X?" "Compare [company] to alternatives." "What does [company] cost?" "Who is this best for?"

Show the company the results. The sales moment: "When buyers ask AI for your category, you do not show up" — or "AI gets your pricing wrong" — or "your competitor shows up because their docs are cleaner."

3
Expert Archive → Agent Tool
Turn a creator's years of content into a job-specific agent that their audience can use
📖 The Idea
Creators have years of valuable content — YouTube, podcasts, newsletters, templates. Right now it makes money via ads and sponsorships. In the agent internet, that archive becomes a tool agents pay to use.

Don't say "we'll turn your brain into AI" — say something specific:
  • → "You have 300 videos about sales. We'll turn them into a tool to rewrite cold emails."
  • → "You have 500 podcast episodes on startups. We'll turn them into a startup idea feedback tool."
  • → "You have a decade of design teardowns. We'll turn them into a landing page critique tool."
How to build it:
Pick the expert: Deep archive + specific audience. B2B works best (cold email, Shopify growth, acquisitions, tax, fitness programming, design).
Collect the archive: Transcribe videos/podcasts, pull newsletters, clean docs.
Tag deeply — don't just throw it in a vector DB. For a sales archive: tag by prospecting, subject line, offer, objection, follow-up, personalization, close. Structure = quality.
Build ONE useful workflow. Sales expert → "Paste your cold email. Agent critiques using the expert's principles, cites source lessons, rewrites, scores, gives one test to run." That's the product.
⭐ Why This Works
The creator already has the distribution and trust — you don't need to do marketing. The audience wants the expertise but the creator can't consult with everyone. This democratizes it at $19–50/month instead of $500/hr consulting. When Cloudflare's payment rails mature: creator gets paid when their knowledge is used. Per request. Automatic.
