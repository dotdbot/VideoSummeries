🎯 The Negotiation Playbook

Step 1 — Research Before You Go

  • Find alternative suppliers — reach out, order samples, compare quality and pricing
  • If you're paying $10 and find similar quality at $8 — that's your leverage
  • Understand material costs, labor costs, and market averages
  • "Line everything out before you enter the conversation — like a game of chess"

Step 2 — Enter With Data, Not Emotions

  • Know what you want before the meeting — price target, payment terms, quality standards
  • Anticipate objections: if they say "materials cost more," you already know what materials actually cost
  • Have competing offers on the table — "we have these really good offers but we want to continue with you"
  • Without preparation, it's "your word against theirs — and you're stuck"

Step 3 — Be Transparent, Not Aggressive

  • "The intention is not to squeeze them" — everyone needs to make money
  • Be direct about concerns: quality issues, pricing gaps, payment needs
  • Frame it as partnership: "How can we get there? What do you need from us to arrive there?"
  • Ask the magic question: "What would it take for you to give me those terms?"
✅ Key Tactic — Trade-Offs
"I could pay slightly more to get more favorable payment terms." Don't just negotiate price — negotiate the full package: price, quality, payment terms, batch sizes, timelines.

🤝 The Actual Meeting (Guangzhou)

  • Client had quality issues with a previous batch of 225K–250K units
  • Client was afraid to place large orders — wanted to split production into smaller batches
  • Opened with transparency: "Our client is scared. They don't trust this won't happen again"
  • Presented competitive research and alternative offers
  • Negotiated in a mix of Chinese and English — huge cultural advantage
  • Result: Better pricing ($0.25/unit reduction on 1M+ pieces = $250K+ savings) plus 60-day payment terms
  • Total estimated impact: $500K–$2M/year
"Nine out of ten times, because people don't understand what's going on on this side of the world... they just accepted that that's how it is and that is the price."

💰 The Business Model

  • Core offering: Help brands expand internationally — produce in China, ship to 100+ countries
  • Cut out the middleman — connect brands directly to factories
  • Fee structure: Charge ~30% of the savings generated in the first year
  • After year one, client banks the full discount
  • Zero risk to client — without you, they wouldn't have those savings
💡 Universally Applicable Model
"Think: how can I help people make more money and take a cut of that?" — This consulting model works in any industry, not just supply chain. If you can generate measurable savings or revenue, charge a percentage.

  • Huge margin gaps exist — same product can be bought for $10 but sold for $50. Most buyers don't research, so they accept $10 when $5–6 is possible
  • Speaking the language is a superpower. Conducting negotiations in Chinese changes the entire dynamic
  • Physical presence matters. Flying to China and meeting face-to-face creates trust that emails never can
  • "Don't just negotiate price — negotiate the full package" — terms, quality, timelines, batch sizes
