🚫 Stay Away From These Four Things

1. Margin

  • Robinhood customers increased margin by about 127% year over year (his reading of recent data), fueling the blow-off tops.
  • Margin turns a 40% drawdown into a 70% one, which needs hundreds of percent to recover, plus margin calls and interest (e.g. 7%) while you lose.
  • Buffett, roughly: if you're smart you don't need it; if you're dumb you shouldn't use it.

2. Call options

  • Weekly and short-dated calls on chips are getting "wrecked week after week" by people betting on the comeback. The bottom comes when they finally give up.

3. Leveraged ETFs

  • DRAM ETF, 2x SanDisk, 2x Micron, 2x Nvidia: a 30% drop can mean ~60% losses. Margin plus leveraged ETFs has wiped some portfolios out entirely.

4. Ignoring bear cases

  • Always run a bear case: what if the CEO leaves or "gets hit by a bus", or the government hurts the business model?
