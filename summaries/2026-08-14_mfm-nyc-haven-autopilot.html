🧠 Best Hiring Advice (Sam & Shawn)

1
Get crystal clear on the role before hiring. Most hiring mistakes start with a vague job spec (often outsourced to ChatGPT or a recruiter). Write down exactly what problem needs solving, what world-class looks like, and what they'll need to change. Generic spec = generic candidate.
2
Bring in an outside interviewer for key hires. Find someone better at interviewing than you. Have them talk to your top 3–4 candidates. The goal isn't just picking the right person — it's watching how they dig in, follow up, and sniff out BS, so you get better yourself.
3
Hire from two pools only. Pool A: someone who has specifically solved this exact problem at another company at a similar stage. Pool B: unproven talent you can "stock-pick" on — a diamond in the rough 10x-er. Build systems to source from both consistently.
4
Map the company that solved your problem, find the person. Don't just hire from "a successful company." Find who was the real shot-caller solving your specific problem at the exact year that company was at your stage. That's the hire.
5
Spend 30% of founder time on recruiting. Most founders with hiring problems spend hours — not days — on it. The best scaling founders spend almost a third of their week on recruiting. If you're not there, it's a root cause of your hiring quality problem.
