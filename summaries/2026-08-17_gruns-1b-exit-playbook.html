3
🧪 Proof — Let the Data Sell for You
Most founders write copy. Grüns wrote evidence.

Grüns' sales pages, Amazon listings, and ads are proof-first, copy-last. No fancy taglines. No lifestyle aspirations. Just data.


Type 1: Clinical Trial Data

  • Ran an actual clinical trial measuring nutrient levels (vitamin C, folate, etc.) before and after taking Grüns
  • Displayed results directly in marketing images — lab-grade credibility at consumer scale

Type 2: Observational Customer Study

  • Surveyed customers: "Has your energy improved? Do you feel better?" → reported the % who said yes
  • This is something any brand can do. Survey your customers, display the results as a stat
  • Example: "87% of users reported improved energy in 30 days" — zero clinical investment required

Type 3: Problem-Proof (the genius move)

Amazon listing stat: "90% of US adults don't meet their recommended daily nutrient intake" — no mention of Grüns.

Also: "61% of Americans experience weekly digestive issues like bloating, abdominal pain." Grüns has lots of fiber. The product is never named as the solution — the reader makes that connection automatically.

This is proof-about-the-problem, not proof-about-the-product. The reader fills in the gap: "I have this problem → this product solves it." No hard sell needed.


What their Amazon listing looks like:

  • Hundreds of reviews
  • Videos of happy customers
  • Clinical stats and observational data embedded in images
  • Competitor comparison charts
  • Problem-proof stats (nutrient gap, digestive issues)

The flywheel: Higher proof → higher conversion rate → more budget for ads → more customers → more reviews → even higher proof. Once you're spinning this wheel faster than competitors, nobody can catch you.
🎯 Your Actionable Playbook (Steal This)
  • Product: Find two converging trends with proof of acquisition/valuation in each. Build the bridge product that sits between them. Spend time making it genuinely great.
  • Pricing: Take the median price in your category and multiply by 2 — that's your starting point for premium positioning.
  • Subscriptions: Add an incentivized first-order price to convert, then let the recurring model run. Build upsells or auto-ship for LTV.
  • Target LTV: Know your CAC. Aim for 10× front-end price as annual LTV. Above 10× = you can outspend every competitor on ads.
  • Proof — start today: Survey your customers. Report the stats. Find 2-3 facts about the problem your product solves and put them front and center in your listing/sales page.
  • Problem-proof: Lead with a stat about the problem, not the product. Let the reader connect the dots. This converts better than any headline.
  • Amazon specifically: Embed proof into every image slot. Reviews, stats, customer videos, problem data. Your competitors are using lifestyle photos — you bring evidence.
