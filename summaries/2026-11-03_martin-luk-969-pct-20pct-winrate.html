⚙️ The System

1. 30-minute routine and 3 scans

  • He trades Hong Kong nights, 9:30 PM to ~4 AM, when New York is open.
  • 30 minutes before the open, three TradingView scans: leaders surfing rising MAs, sector themes moving together, and pre-market gappers. No automated buy alerts.
  • Never trades slow stocks — a 1% stop only works on names with explosive daily range.

2. Setups (long side)

  • Mix: 65.9% pullbacks, 17.8% high-tight flags, 14.0% episodic pivots, 2.3% breakouts.
  • The "handshake" pullback: (1) a leader makes a powerful leg up on heavy volume; (2) orderly 3–5-day pullback into the rising 9/21-day EMA and anchored VWAP as volume dries up; (3) enter when the intraday pullback undercuts the prior low and reclaims the MA, stop under the day's low.

  • Episodic pivots: opening-range breakouts on an 8% gap are too violent. He waits 15–30 minutes for the shakeout, then buys the intraday AVWAP reclaim, stop under ½ ADR.

3. Short side

  • When a parabolic leader cracks or the market enters a Weinstein Stage 4, he flips the pullback model upside down.

4. Execution: 1-min / 5-min and three AVWAP lines

  • Anchors: (1) the catalyst/earnings gap day, (2) the recent swing high, (3) the pullback pivot low.
  • Step 1: let the morning shakeout flush weak hands below the prior low. Step 2: enter when price reclaims the intraday AVWAP and breaks the opening-range high. Step 3: hard stop under the flush candle, capped at ½ ADR.
  • A 1–2% stop on a stock that moves 8% a day: one normal expansion = 4–5x risk by the close.
  • If stopped out and the stock reclaims AVWAP 20 minutes later, he re-buys without ego.

5. Losing streaks: the inchworm model

  • Tendler: long-term compounding is decided by your C-game tail — the worst emotional moments — not your A-game peak. Raise the floor by cutting off emotional mistakes.

6. Sizing, trims and sell rules

  • Risk 0.3–0.5% per trade.
  • At 3–5R (usually day 1–2) trim 10–15% and move the stop to breakeven — the trade is now risk-free; keep 85–90% for the move.

  • Nuances: on 250–350% margin he sells into strength in 15–20% or even 50% tranches; on parabolic volume blow-offs (2–3x daily volume) he exits 100%; shorts are covered into weakness in 2–3 days.
