⚙️ The Complete Business System
Step 1
Lead Generation with AI
Tools: Apollo (verified business emails) · PhantomBuster (scrape IG/LinkedIn) · Clay (enrich + personalize at scale)

Use Claude to write personalized cold DMs mentioning something specific about the prospect's business. AI can find leads, write outreach, and even help deliver the service itself.

Volume target: 50–100 DMs/day. Expect ~5–10% reply rate. The skill isn't the service — it's finding people and starting conversations.
Step 2
Book & Run Sales Calls via Calendly
Set up a Calendly event with custom screening questions (revenue, tools they use, what they're struggling with). Block unavailable times. Connect Google Calendar + Google Meet for auto-booking. Send Calendly link directly in DMs to interested leads.

Sales call structure:
1. Build rapport — talk like a normal person, ask about their business
2. Ask questions — what's the problem? Why no website yet? What's failed before? Let them talk
3. Present your service — focus on the outcome, not the tools. Don't say "I edit videos." Say "I help you get more views without making more content."
4. State the price confidently — "For this, I charge $2,000." Then stop talking. Silence = confidence
5. Handle objections — "What's holding you back?" Bring it back to the result. Most objections = uncertainty, so your job is to minimize uncertainty

Nervous? Do your first 1–2 clients free or discounted. Get a case study. Then sell with proof — confidence comes from clarity.
Step 3
Build a Professional Website with Claude Code
Use Claude Code (code tab) with a provided prompt template. Copy a brand kit from Canva → upload PDF → tell Claude to redesign the site following brand guidelines → instant premium look with consistent fonts, colors, spacing.

Host free on Vercel in under 30 seconds. Buy a custom domain and connect it for professionalism. Embed your Calendly calendar at the bottom so visitors can book directly from the site.

Website structure (Evan + WGMY examples):
→ Headline + transformation statement (who you help + what result you deliver)
→ Sub-headline explaining what you do
→ Book a call CTA (primary goal of the entire site)
→ Video explaining your service
→ Client logos / who you've worked with
→ How it works (3 simple steps)
→ Case studies (clickable stories: problem → solution → result)
→ Embedded Calendly calendar

Warning: Don't spend weeks perfecting the site. People trick themselves into "being productive" on design when they're really avoiding the discomfort of outreach. Start sending messages before the site is perfect.
Step 4
Build a Professional Social Profile
When leads receive a DM from you, they check your profile. It must signal: A) real person, B) you do what you claim, C) you're professional.

4 profile elements:
→ Professional headshot or logo
→ One-line bio stating exactly what you do and for whom
→ Link to your website in bio
→ 3 pinned posts: your work, case studies, results

Use your personal account — people buy from people. Being a young college student is an advantage, not a liability.
Step 5
Deliver the Service
Onboarding: Collect everything upfront — logos, logins, brand assets, photos, menu/content. The smoother your onboarding, the better your first impression. It should feel like you've done this before.

Set clear expectations: Week 1 = setup, Week 2 = first deliverable, Week 3 = optimization. No confusion = no stress.

Don't know how to do the work? Good. Look it up. Watch YouTube tutorials. Ask Claude. Your client is not expecting delivery in 24 hours — you have at least a week to learn and get a first version done. Clients only care about the result, not how you got there.

Key reframe: School teaches you to study 4 years before you're ready. That's backwards. You learn by solving real problems for real people.
Step 6
Referrals, Case Studies & Inbound Content
After delivering results: "Do you know anyone else who would benefit from something like this?" — referrals close easiest because they already trust you via the person who referred them.

Build the case study: What was their problem? How did you solve it? What was the result? Example: "Grew creator from 10K to 100K views using short-form content." Add to website, social profile, and outreach messages.

Create content around the case study — post on Twitter, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn. Title: "Here's how I got this client 1 million views in 30 days." Walk through the process with a simple Loom video. No fancy camera needed.

This does two things: builds authority (people see you as an expert) + generates inbound leads (people find you instead of you finding them). Once you have referrals + case studies + content, clients come to you — everything compounds.

🧠 Core Mindset Shifts

⚡ Start selling before you feel ready

Most people think: "I need to learn everything first." That's backwards. You learn by getting clients and doing the work as you go. The clients don't care how you did it — only the result matters.

💰 The skill that makes money is selling, not the service

Design, coding, editing — these are all valuable. But the skill that converts to income is your ability to find people, start conversations, and close deals. Selling is the universal multiplier.

🚫 Perfecting the website is avoidance

People spend weeks "building the website" when they're really avoiding the discomfort of reaching out to strangers. Recognize this pattern. Send outreach messages even before the site is live.

🎯 Your first client doesn't need to be paid

Do 1–2 clients free or at a discount. Get a case study with a real result. Once you have proof, selling becomes dramatically easier because you're no longer asking them to trust a stranger — you're showing evidence.

📈 One client should become many

Deliver results → ask for referral → build case study → create content about it. This compound loop turns one happy client into a referral engine + authority signal + inbound lead generator. The business shifts from "always hunting" to "clients finding you."
