I Went to China to Negotiate With a Supplier (Saved $1,000,000/Year)

Speaker helps brands negotiate with Chinese manufacturers. Trip to Guangzhou.

Key tactics:
- Research before negotiating: find alternative suppliers, order samples, understand market pricing
- Create leverage: if you pay $10 and find similar quality at $8, that's your leverage
- Understand labor costs, material costs, average pricing
- Enter conversation with everything lined out — "like a game of chess"
- Don't squeeze suppliers — find reasonable margins
- Be transparent: share concerns openly, build trust

Meeting highlights:
- Client had quality issues with previous batch (225K-250K units)
- Client afraid to place large orders due to past problems
- Negotiated better pricing by showing competitive offers
- Result: estimated $500K-$2M/year savings for client
- Also secured better payment terms (60 days post-production)

Business model insight:
- Position yourself to help clients save money
- Charge percentage of savings (e.g., 30% of first year discount)
- Low risk for client — they wouldn't have savings without you
- After first year, client banks full discount

China business tips:
- Suppliers may eventually become competitors (DJI example)
- Many manufacturers now reaching end consumers directly
- Understanding China side creates massive leverage
- Ask "what would it take?" to get desired terms
- Be firm but fair — everyone needs to make money
