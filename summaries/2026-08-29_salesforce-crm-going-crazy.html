📊 Earnings Breakdown — "Meh Report, Huge Catalyst"

  • R&D up 14%, Sales & Marketing up 12%, Total OpEx up 12% — all outstripping gross profit growth. Only GNA dropped (1%), but it's a small line item.
  • Net Income up 87%, EPS up 119% — but almost entirely due to a $2.6B one-off gain on strategic investments. Strip that out: the underlying picture is "C-grade" per Jeremy's grading system.
  • Previous earnings grade: C — expense problem flagged 3 months ago; it has worsened.
  • Free Cash Flow: $1.1B, Operating Cash Flow: $1.3B — genuinely healthy numbers, no concern there.
  • Guidance: ~$200M above analyst expectations — not a blowout, but for a beaten-down stock, "better than expected" is all that matters.
⚠️ The Core Risk
Total cost of revenues jumped from +13% (last Q) to +18% this quarter. Expenses are accelerating faster than revenue. This is the #1 thing to watch in the next 2–3 quarters.
